The control plane on your laptop

Compatibility is infrastructure.

The bridge is not another chat app. It is the translation and resilience layer between opinionated AI clients and the endpoint your organization chooses.

↔

Protocol transcoding

Responses, Chat Completions, Anthropic Messages, tools, thinking blocks, and SSE events move between dialects without changing the client.

M

Model aliasing

Stable client-facing names route to configurable enterprise model slots while preserving agent tool behavior.

R

Stream resilience

Connect, idle, and total timeouts combine with bounded retries and an optional non-stream fallback.

K

Credential isolation

Clients can use a local placeholder while the bridge holds the real upstream credential in protected per-user state.

∞

Capacity controls

Body limits, in-flight ceilings, model-specific concurrency, and backpressure prevent one caller from owning the machine.

◎

Routing evidence

Structured metrics expose selected models, latency, retries, token usage, and estimated savings without running inference locally.

Two dialects in. One upstream out.

Clients keep speaking native.

The bridge absorbs the incompatibility tax. Your tools keep their expected APIs; your upstream stays OpenAI-compatible and centrally governed.
